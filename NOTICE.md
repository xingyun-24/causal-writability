# Provenance

The raw-video training backbone is derived from the Seriality Gap codebase.
The vendored Free Fall DiffSynth source comes from DiffSynth-Studio commit
`afd101f3452c9ecae0c87b79adfa2e22d65ffdc3`.

This repository contains the Spring, Pendulum, Free Fall and pretrained-Wan
experiments for Causal Writability in Video Models. Individual runtime notices
and upstream license files remain in their original task directories.
